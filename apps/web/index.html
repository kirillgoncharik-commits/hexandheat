The art of alchemy

Collect matching symbols anywhere on the 6 × 5 board. Winning symbols disappear and new symbols cascade into their place. Potion events increase the cauldron multiplier during this round.

Round values

The round win accumulates during the chain. The final result stays visible. The cauldron returns to ×1 for each independent round. Character reactions do not change results.

Controls

Spin or Space starts a round. Bet amount changes its size. Fast shortens the presentation. Replay last replays the previous preview round. Sound and motion options are in Settings.

Sample base rules and paytable

8 or more of one identical ID pays anywhere. L1–L5 and H1–H4 are nine different symbols. Groups pay with the current multiplier, then all visible Potion activate and disappear with winning symbols. P1/P2/P3 add +1/+2/+3 for following cascades only. On a non-paying board, Potion stays inactive. One paid sample starts at ×1.

Whole-round cap: 5000×. On cap the chain stops on its last authoritative board. Technical limit: 64 cascades; exceeding it is an error. 100 payout units = 1× bet; currency uses exact six-decimal units. Values below a cent remain visible.
